Yes: transfer still helps after context is modelled. Accuracy stays near 64 to 65, because every gain comes from rare classes.

Test macro-F1, mean of three seeds. The gaps are about the size of the seed spread, so every difference here is computed within a seed and then averaged. Baseline 45.2. Transfer alone plus 1.85. Context alone plus 1.15. Transfer on top of context plus 1.34: that is the research question, and the answer is yes. Both together plus 2.49, close to the sum, so the two factors do different jobs. All twelve per-seed differences are positive. Three seeds show direction; we do not claim significance.

Likely questions. Why is accuracy flat: neutral is 48 percent of the test set and every system already scores 78 to 80 F1 on it. Is plus 1.85 significant: we claim direction only, positive in 3 of 3 seeds. Why exclude neutral in Stage I: the specification requires it and MELD has its own neutral. GoEmotions is multi-label: we keep examples with exactly one non-neutral label. Why 24 turns: memory; longer dialogues are split into windows that never cross a dialogue. Why is macro-F1 under 50: fear and disgust have 50 and 68 test utterances; weighted-F1 for the ensemble is 65.1. Tuning: a 15-variant sweep on M4 found nothing better than the default recipe on dev. Reproducibility: all 52 runs retrained from scratch with one command matched the reported metrics exactly.
